Rules & staffing

Hard limit across all rooms, occupied or empty. Includes assigned empty beds. 4090 ↔ 4061 is one step.
Enter the census, then optimize.
Ratio limits: any IMC → maximum 3 patients; MS-only → maximum 4. Reserved empty beds also use capacity, but never count as patients for workload balance.
Preferences: cover every patient and avoid single-patient assignments first. For bed meeting, preserve admission capacity next: one open bed prefers IMC-capable; two open beds prefer 1 IMC-capable + 1 MS, then 2 IMC-capable. Within those goals, favor sensible IMC/MS groupings and shorter walks. The search can skip rooms.
Manual workflow: select an RN, choose available rooms, then Lock RN. Optimization replaces unlocked assignments and preserves each locked RN exactly. Release assignment frees one RN’s rooms. Clear unlocked assignments keeps all locks.
Nearby empty beds are reserved when capacity and the hard maximum span allow; otherwise open slots remain. Validate reports unassigned patients and separately lists unreserved empty beds. The empty-room setting is a preference, not a safety limit.
